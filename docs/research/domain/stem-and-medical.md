# GrantResearcher: STEM & Medical Research Grants

## UK RESEARCH COUNCILS — STEM SPECIFICS

### EPSRC (Engineering & Physical Sciences)
- **Standard grants**: Up to £2M FEC, up to 5 years. Currently paused for programme grants in energy/decarbonisation, manufacturing/circular economy, quantum technologies.
- **New Investigator Awards**: For researchers who have not held a major grant. Currently paused.
- **Programme grants**: Large, long-term (up to 6 years). Strategic areas only.
- **Fellowship schemes**: Various career stages.
- **Budget**: Part of UKRI's ~£8bn annual allocation.

### BBSRC (Biological Sciences)
- **Standard Research Grants**: Up to £2M FEC, up to 5 years. 80% FEC funded. Responsive mode Spotlight areas for strategic targeting. Round 1 2026 closed Jan 2026, future rounds paused during UKRI restructuring.
- **Follow-on Fund (FoF)**: Bridges gap between bioscience research and commercialisation. Must draw on current/prior BBSRC funding.
- **New Investigator Awards**: Currently paused.

### MRC (Medical Research Council)
- **Core budget**: ~£602M for 2025-26.
- **Investigator-led research grants** (formerly project/programme grants): Paused since Dec 2025, expected to reopen summer 2026.
- **New Investigator Research Grants**: Paused.
- **Partnership grants**: Paused.
- **Translational funding**: Experimental medicine, developmental pathway funding — also paused.
- **Doctoral Training Partnerships (DTPs)**: Still active, allocated to universities.

### STFC (Science & Technology Facilities)
- **Budget**: £835-842M flat, seeking £162M savings by 2029-30.
- **Particle physics, astronomy, nuclear physics**: 15-30% cuts in new grants.
- **Facilities**: Diamond Light Source facing 15% cuts.
- **PhD studentships**: Allocated through universities.

### NERC (Natural Environment)
- **Grants on the Web (GOTW)**: gotww.nerc.ac.uk — searchable database of all NERC-funded projects.
- **Standard grants, large grants, fellowships**: Available.

### Cross-council
- **Future Leaders Fellowships (FLF)**: 4-7 years, for ambitious researchers. Cross-disciplinary. Round 11 launched Jan 2026.
- **UKRI Frontier Research Grants**: New cross-council mechanism.
- **Studentships**: At least £21,805/year stipend + £5,238 tuition (2026/27).

**Critical context (April 2026)**: UKRI is undergoing major restructuring. MRC, BBSRC, EPSRC have paused key responsive-mode grants. Budget is rising to ~£10bn/year by 2030 but curiosity-driven funding is flat. This makes alternative funding sources more important than ever.

## MEDICAL RESEARCH CHARITIES (150+ AMRC members)

The Association of Medical Research Charities (AMRC) has 150+ members collectively providing ~£1.6bn/year, representing 36-47% of all publicly funded medical research in the UK. 90%+ goes to universities.

### Tier 1: Major medical charities (>£50M/year research spend)
- **Cancer Research UK (CRUK)**: World's largest cancer charity. Grant types include clinical trials, fellowships, programme grants (>4 years), project grants (<4 years), studentships, lectureships, equipment, units.
- **British Heart Foundation (BHF)**: £108M+ in research grants. Cardiovascular disease.
- **Wellcome Trust**: £29.1bn endowment. Biomedical research, medical humanities, public health. PhD fellowships, discovery research, career development.
- **Alzheimer's Research UK**: Received £79M government boost for early detection research.

### Tier 2: Disease-specific charities
- **Parkinson's UK** — Movement disorders research
- **Arthritis Research UK / Versus Arthritis** — Musculoskeletal
- **Diabetes UK** — Diabetes research
- **Stroke Association** — Stroke research
- **MS Society** — Multiple sclerosis
- **Epilepsy Research UK** — Epilepsy
- **Kidney Research UK** — Renal disease
- **Breast Cancer Now** — Breast cancer
- **Leukaemia UK / Bloodwise** — Blood cancers
- **Meningitis Research Foundation** — Meningitis & septicaemia
- **Motor Neurone Disease Association** — MND/ALS
- **Cystic Fibrosis Trust** — CF research
- **Scar Free Foundation** — Scarring/wound healing
- **CORE / Guts UK** — Digestive disorders
- **British Liver Trust** — Liver disease
- **British Lung Foundation / Asthma + Lung UK** — Respiratory
- **Mental Health Foundation** — Mental health research
- **CCLG** — Children and Young People's Cancer

### Tier 3: Rare disease / niche
- **Muscular Dystrophy UK** — Neuromuscular conditions
- **Rett Syndrome Research Trust**
- **Tuberous Sclerosis Association**
- **Huntington's Disease Association**
- **Scoliosis Research Foundation**
- Hundreds more — each with own grant schemes

### Key insight
AMRC members that award grants via open competition are eligible for the **Charity Research Support Fund (CRSF)**, which adds up to 28p to every charity pound invested in universities. This makes charity-funded research cost-effective for universities — an important signal for GrantResearcher to surface.

## NIHR (National Institute for Health & Care Research)

UK's largest funder of applied health and care research. Funded by Department of Health.

- **Research for Patient Benefit (RfPB)**: Including dedicated calls for nurses and midwives (~£3M, 16 projects in recent round).
- **Programme Grants for Applied Research**: Large, multi-year.
- **Health Technology Assessment (HTA)**: Evaluating interventions.
- **Fellowships**: Pre-doctoral, doctoral, post-doctoral, senior.
- **Internships**: Short-term research experience.
- **Public Health Research Programme**
- **Health Services & Delivery Research**
- **Invention for Innovation (i4i)**: Translational.
- **Clinical Research Network**: Infrastructure funding.

**Devolved equivalents:**
- **Scotland**: Chief Scientist Office
- **Wales**: Health and Care Research Wales
- **Northern Ireland**: HSC R&D

## ENGINEERING-SPECIFIC

- **Royal Academy of Engineering**: Research chairs, fellowships, industry partnerships, enterprise fellowships, frontiers grants. Aims to bridge academia-industry.
- **Institution of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE)**: Various awards.
- **Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET)**: Research grants, travel awards.
- **Royal Aeronautical Society**: Research grants.
- **Institute of Physics (IOP)**: Research grants, early career awards.
- **Royal Society of Chemistry (RSC)**: Research fund, mobility grants, journals grants.
