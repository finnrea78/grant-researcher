# GrantResearcher: The Weird, Wonderful & Overlooked

*The grants most researchers never find.*

---

## SPACE & AEROSPACE

- **ESA Post-Graduate Research Grant**: €5,000 for humanities/social science research on ESA history. Archival research in Florence. Open to postgrads and early-career academics in history, social sciences, humanities.
- **ESA Co-funded Research**: Up to 50%, up to €90K for PhD or postdoc. Novel space-related research. Via OSIP platform, year-round.
- **ESA Academy**: Conference sponsorship, short course scholarships, academic scholarships for students in member states.
- **EUSPA Grants**: Space-enabled services for higher education, fisheries, agriculture. EU Space Regulation funding.
- **UK Space Agency**: Various grants via UKRI/STFC.

---

## MIDWIFERY & MATERNITY

- **Iolanthe Midwifery Trust**: Research fellowships, Dora Opoku Awards (specifically for Black and Brown midwives), general awards, return to practice, service improvement. UK midwives and students.
- **Wellbeing of Women / RCM Entry Level Scholarship**: Up to £20,000 for midwife-led research. Must be midwife and RCM member.
- **NIHR RfPB Under-represented Disciplines**: Specific calls for nurse/midwife-led research (£3M, 16 projects in recent round). No PhD required.
- **Nightingale Fund**: Post-registration education including PhDs. Named after Florence Nightingale.
- **Barbers' Company Clinical Nursing Scholarship**: For clinical practice nurses/midwives.
- **Florence Nightingale Digital Fellowship**: Research-focused digital health.
- **King's College Hospital Nurses' League**: CPD grants for league members.

---

## FOOD & GASTRONOMY

- **Getty Anne Willan & Mark Cherniavsky Gastronomy Collection Grant**: Research using Getty's culinary history collection.
- **Oxford Symposium on Food and Cookery**: Small grants.
- **Nuffield Farming Scholarships**: Travel/study awards for farming/food.
- **AHDB**: Statutory levy board for agriculture. Research funding.

---

## TRADE & PROFESSION-LINKED

- **Leverhulme Trade Charities Trust**: Grants for people connected to travelling sales representatives, pharmacists, or grocers (or their relatives). Sister to the Leverhulme Trust.
- **Vegetarian Charity**: Up to £500 for vegetarians/vegans under 26.
- **Sir Richard Stapley Educational Trust**: £400-£1,000 annual grants for any subject.
- **Worshipful Company of [various]**: Many Livery Companies of the City of London have grant schemes. E.g., Worshipful Company of Dyers, Goldsmiths, etc. — often obscure but funded.

---

## CONFLICT, MIGRATION & HUMAN RIGHTS

- **Gerda Henkel Foundation — Scholars at Risk**: Scholarships for threatened/refugee academics from crisis regions. Historical humanities focus. Subsistence (6 months), doctoral (2 years), research (1 year).
- **Gerda Henkel — Forced Migration programme**: Special programme alongside Democracy and Lost Cities.
- **Cara (Council for At-Risk Academics)**: UK-based. Fellowships for academics at risk.
- **IIE Scholar Rescue Fund**: Emergency fellowships for threatened scholars worldwide.

---

## CULTURAL HERITAGE & CONSERVATION

- **Getty Foundation Keeping It Modern / Conserving Canvas**: Conservation management plans for modern architecture and paintings.
- **Gerda Henkel — Patrimonies**: Cultural heritage preservation in crisis regions.
- **Heritage Fund (National Lottery)**: UK heritage projects.
- **Arcadia Fund**: Endangered cultural heritage and ecosystems.

---

## AFRICAN ART & DIASPORA (Will's world)

- **Getty Connecting Art Histories**: Funded "Black Mediterranean" at I Tatti — postdoc fellowships for scholars from Africa exploring African influence on European art.
- **Getty AAAHI**: African American Art History Initiative. Whitney & Lee Kaplan Collection Grant.
- **Gerda Henkel Lisa Maskell Fellowships**: Young humanities scholars in Africa since 2014.
- **Ford Foundation**: Museum for African Art, Museum of West African Art (Benin artefacts digitisation).
- **Mellon Foundation**: Conservation Initiative in African Art ($1.5M to VMFA).
- **Smithsonian**: Various fellowships for African art research.
- **Paul Mellon Centre**: British art history — could include colonial/imperial connections to African art.
- **British Academy international schemes**: UK-Africa research collaboration.

---

## ENVIRONMENT & SUSTAINABILITY

- **Environmental Grassroots Alliance (EGA)**: Environmental nonprofits, sustainability-focused funders.
- **National Geographic Society**: Research & exploration grants. All disciplines with fieldwork component.
- **WWF**: Conservation research.
- **Earthwatch**: Field research partnerships.

---

## SCOTTISH-SPECIFIC

- **Royal Society of Edinburgh**: Research grants, fellowships. Scotland's national academy. Cultural, economic, social wellbeing of Scotland.
- **Chief Scientist Office (Scotland)**: Health research.
- **Carnegie Trust for the Universities of Scotland**: Research grants, PhD scholarships.
- **Funding Scotland (funding.scot)**: Scotland-specific database.

---

## WELSH-SPECIFIC

- **WCVA (Wales Council for Voluntary Action)**: Funding portal.
- **Health and Care Research Wales**: Welsh health research equivalent of NIHR.
- **Arts Council of Wales**: Welsh arts funding.

---

## NORTHERN IRELAND-SPECIFIC

- **NICVA**: NI funding database.
- **HSC R&D (Health & Social Care R&D)**: NI health research.
- **NI Executive Grants Portal**: govfundingpublic.nics.gov.uk

---

## LOCAL & REGIONAL (ENGLAND)

- **Combined Authority grants**: Greater Manchester, West Midlands, etc.
- **Local Enterprise Partnerships (LEPs)**: Various innovation grants.
- **Mayoral funds**: Various.
- **University internal funds**: Almost every university has its own small grants, seed funding, equipment grants. These aren't publicly discoverable but could be a future feature.

---

## PRIZES & AWARDS (often overlooked as "grants")

Many prizes come with research funding attached:
- **Philip Leverhulme Prizes**: £100K for early-career researchers (any discipline)
- **Wolfson Research Merit Awards**: Salary enhancement for outstanding researchers
- **Royal Society Wolfson Fellowships**: Up to 5 years
- **British Academy Wolfson Professorships**: Senior scholars
- **Blavatnik Awards for Young Scientists**: $100K (UK awards)
- **L'Oréal-UNESCO For Women in Science**: Fellowships for women scientists
- **Rolex Awards for Enterprise**: Projects benefiting communities/environment

---

## THE ABSOLUTE WEIRDEST

These exist and a good grant finder should know about them:

- **Vegetarian Charity**: £500 for vegan/vegetarian researchers under 26
- **Leverhulme Trade Charities Trust**: Must be related to a grocer, pharmacist, or travelling salesperson
- **ESA history grant**: €5K to research the history of European rocketry in Italian archives
- **Whitney & Lee Kaplan Grant**: Specifically for research using one collection of African American visual culture at Getty
- **Abbeyfield Research Foundation**: Older people research — may be dormant (last call 2020)
- **Private Physiotherapy Education Foundation**: Pump-priming research, physiotherapy only
- **Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in Respiratory Care**: £500 CPD + £200 travel, members only
- **Hakluyt Society**: History of exploration and travel
- **British Scoliosis Research Foundation**: Scoliosis only

---

## PRODUCT IMPLICATION

Every single one of these represents a *discovery failure* in the current system. A researcher with scoliosis research interests doesn't know the British Scoliosis Research Foundation exists. A midwife doesn't know about Iolanthe. An art historian studying African diaspora art doesn't know about Getty's AAAHI or Gerda Henkel's Lisa Maskell Fellowships.

**This is exactly where GrantResearcher creates value.** The agent takes a researcher's profile and matches against *all of this*, including the long tail that no human could manually track.
