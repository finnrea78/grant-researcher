## 1. Project Framing

The Leverhulme Research Fellowship is a shorter, lower-budget version of the Major Research Fellowship (MRF) — 3 to 24 months at up to £70,000, targeting experienced researchers whose **teaching and administrative duties have significantly impacted their research in the preceding 3 years**. The scheme requires applicants to demonstrate specifically that their duties have constrained research in the recent past — this is a stronger eligibility criterion than the MRF, but Rea meets it convincingly. Twenty-five years of teaching at Leeds, Director of Graduate School (2008–13), Programme Leader (2014–21), and ongoing PhD supervision of 4 active students creates an unambiguous record of institutional duty limiting research time.

Unlike the MRF, the Research Fellowship can be shorter (as little as 3 months) and involves a minimum 75% time commitment during the award period. Up to 100 fellowships are awarded annually, making it more accessible than the MRF. The scheme is currently closed; the next deadline is expected in autumn 2026.

## 7. Budget Considerations

Up to £70,000 covers replacement teaching costs and research expenses. At Leeds Senior Lecturer scale (approximately £50,000–£65,000/year), a 12-month fellowship at the maximum may not fully cover replacement costs without institutional top-up. A shorter period (6 months, ~£35,000) is more financially clean. Research expenses within the total can cover fieldwork travel, archive visits, and associated research costs.
