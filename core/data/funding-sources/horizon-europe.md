## Scheme: ERC Advanced Grant

- **Status:** open
- **Deadline:** TBC — check https://erc.europa.eu/apply-grant/advanced-grants for 2026 call deadline (typically opens spring, deadline August/September annually)
- **Amount:** Up to €2,500,000 over 5 years. Additional up to €1,000,000 for start-up costs in special circumstances; up to €2,000,000 if PI relocating from a non-associated third country.
- **Duration:** 5 years
- **Career stage:** established
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: yes (UK is a Horizon Europe associated country — UK researchers are fully eligible)
  - Nationality restrictions: none — but host institution must be in an EU Member State or associated country (UK qualifies)
  - Prior grant restrictions: none stated
  - Other: Applicant must be the sole PI. Must have a track record of significant research achievements in the preceding 10 years.
- **Themes/priorities:** Bottom-up — any field of research. No predetermined thematic priorities. Assessed on excellence of researcher and project only.
- **What they're looking for:** Established research leaders with a recognised track record of significant and original contributions. Projects should be high risk/high gain frontier research with transformative potential.
- **Application process:** 1. Identify host institution. 2. Contact National Contact Point. 3. Write and submit proposal via EU submission service. 4. Ethics review if funded.
- **URL:** https://erc.europa.eu/apply-grant/advanced-grants
- **Notes:** Most prestigious individual research grant in Europe — €2.5m over 5 years. Highly competitive. UK researchers are eligible post-association. This is relevant to Rea given career stage, but no prior ERC experience is a noted weakness — consult UKRO (UK Research and Innovation's European team) before applying.

## Scheme: ERC Synergy Grant

- **Status:** open
- **Deadline:** TBC — check https://erc.europa.eu/apply-grant/synergy-grants
- **Amount:** Up to €10,000,000
- **Duration:** 6 years
- **Career stage:** any
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: yes (Horizon Europe associated country)
  - Nationality restrictions: none
  - Prior grant restrictions: none stated
  - Other: 2–4 researchers combining complementary skills at different institutions. All PIs must be at eligible institutions.
- **Themes/priorities:** Any field — bottom-up. Must address challenges requiring genuine interdisciplinary synergy.
- **What they're looking for:** Small groups of researchers with complementary skills tackling ambitious problems that could not be solved by a single team
- **Application process:** see URL
- **URL:** https://erc.europa.eu/apply-grant/synergy-grants
- **Notes:** Potential route if combined with a social anthropologist, postcolonial historian, or Nigerian-based researcher. Very high prestige and value but very competitive.

## Scheme: HERA (Humanities in the European Research Area) — Collaborative Calls

- **Status:** closed
- **Deadline:** No current call open (as of 2026-03-29)
- **Amount:** €15,500,000 total for most recent call (2023); distributed across ~10 projects
- **Duration:** 3 years per call cycle
- **Career stage:** any
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: yes (UK participation via AHRC as a HERA member)
  - Nationality restrictions: researchers must be based in a HERA member country
  - Prior grant restrictions: none stated
  - Other: Collaborative — must include partners from at least 3 different HERA countries. Multi-national team required.
- **Themes/priorities:** Themed calls — themes rotate every 3–4 years. Most recent (2023–2026): "Crisis – Perspectives from the Humanities" (environmental, migration, AI, geopolitical themes). Previous (2019–2022): "Public Spaces: Culture and Integration in Europe."
- **What they're looking for:** Transnational collaborative humanities research teams addressing the current call theme. Typically 3–5 partner institutions from different countries.
- **Application process:** Monitor heranet.info for new calls. Contact HERA Executive Office — hera@hera-net.eu. When a call opens, apply via national funding body (AHRC for UK partners).
- **URL:** https://heranet.info/funding/current-funding-opportunities/
- **Notes:** No active call at time of harvest. Calls are infrequent (every 3–4 years). A project on African/diasporic heritage in European public spaces, or decolonisation of European museum collections, could fit a future HERA theme. Monitor for next call announcement.
