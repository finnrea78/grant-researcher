## Scheme: Major Research Fellowships

- **Status:** open
- **Deadline:** 2026-05-08 (4pm)
- **Amount:** Replacement teaching costs (salary replacement from lecturer scale entry point at applicant's institution) + up to £6,000/year research expenses
- **Duration:** 2–3 years, commencing at beginning of academic year
- **Career stage:** established
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: yes (research degree-awarding institution)
  - Nationality restrictions: none stated
  - Prior grant restrictions: previous Major Research Fellowship holders are ineligible
  - Other: "Well-established, distinguished researchers in the humanities and social sciences." No specific professional rank required — "scholarship at the highest level" is the criterion. Senior Lecturers are eligible.
- **Themes/priorities:** Humanities and social sciences broadly. No thematic restrictions.
- **What they're looking for:** Senior researchers whose routine institutional duties prevent completion of a major programme of original research. The fellowship buys out teaching and admin time.
- **Application process:** Online Grants Management System. Three external referees required (no institutional colleagues). Decisions expected December 2026. No individual feedback for unsuccessful applications.
- **URL:** https://www.leverhulme.ac.uk/major-research-fellowships
- **Notes:** One of the most prestigious humanities fellowships in the UK. Applicants need not be professors — Senior Lecturers are eligible. Current deadline is 8 May 2026 — OPEN NOW.

## Scheme: Research Fellowships

- **Status:** closed
- **Deadline:** 2025-11-04 (closed — next round opens 2026, deadline TBC)
- **Amount:** Up to £70,000 (replacement costs + research expenses)
- **Duration:** 3–24 months
- **Career stage:** established
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: yes (must hold a post extending beyond the fellowship)
  - Nationality restrictions: must be UK resident at time of application
  - Prior grant restrictions: none stated
  - Other: Must be in a teaching/admin-heavy role — those already in research-only posts are ineligible. Researchers on short-term contracts ineligible. Priority given to applicants whose duties have significantly impacted research in the preceding 3 years.
- **Themes/priorities:** Any discipline. Does not fund studies of human/animal disease or clinical medical applications.
- **What they're looking for:** Experienced researchers whose teaching and administrative duties have limited their research output. Minimum 75% time commitment during the award.
- **Application process:** Online Grants Management System. Institutional approval required (five working days before deadline). ~100 fellowships awarded annually.
- **URL:** https://www.leverhulme.ac.uk/research-fellowships
- **Notes:** Closed 4 November 2025. Decisions April 2026. Commencement window June 2026–May 2027. International research projects welcome; overseas visits >3 months require hosting institution confirmation.

## Scheme: Research Project Grants

- **Status:** upcoming
- **Deadline:** 2026-07-01 (detailed applications, invited only); 2026-09-01 (second round)
- **Amount:** Up to £500,000
- **Duration:** Up to 5 years
- **Career stage:** any
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: yes (PI must be at UK university or eligible independent research institute)
  - Nationality restrictions: overseas co-applicants permitted
  - Prior grant restrictions: no continuation funding for existing grants
  - Other: Minimum 75% of budget must support research staff. At least one person must work ≥50% time throughout.
- **Themes/priorities:** Any discipline. Does NOT fund: medical/clinical research, policy-influence projects, commercial applications, database assembly, advocacy, networking, or skills development.
- **What they're looking for:** Original research with clear research questions. Up to 3 co-applicants.
- **Application process:** Two-stage. Outline applications currently paused (resuming autumn 2026). Detailed applications by invitation only; 9 months preparation time. Decisions October and December 2026.
- **URL:** https://www.leverhulme.ac.uk/research-project-grants
- **Notes:** Outline stage is paused — not accepting new outline applications at time of harvest. Earliest project start dates: April, November, December 2026.
