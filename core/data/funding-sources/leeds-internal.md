## Scheme: Faculty Impact Acceleration Account (IAA)

- **Status:** TBC — contact Faculty of Arts, Humanities and Cultures Research Office
- **Deadline:** rolling (typically)
- **Amount:** varies (small grants for impact and public engagement activities)
- **Duration:** short-term
- **Career stage:** any
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: yes (University of Leeds AHC faculty and staff only)
  - Nationality restrictions: Leeds staff only
  - Prior grant restrictions: none
  - Other: Must link to existing Leeds research. For rapid-response impact projects, public engagement, and knowledge exchange activities.
- **Themes/priorities:** Impact and public engagement activities linked to existing research. Not for primary research — for translation and dissemination of completed work.
- **What they're looking for:** Projects extending the reach and impact of active or completed research into non-academic audiences
- **Application process:** Internal process — contact AHC Research Office
- **URL:** https://ahc.leeds.ac.uk/
- **Notes:** Good mechanism for smaller public engagement activities, e.g. events around Yoruba art in UK collections or UK dissemination activities linked to the John Randle Museum project.

## Scheme: Leeds Arts and Humanities Research Institute (LAHRI) — Seed Funding

- **Status:** TBC — contact LAHRI directly
- **Deadline:** TBC
- **Amount:** varies (typically small seed funding for networks, seminars, and workshops)
- **Duration:** short-term
- **Career stage:** any
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: yes (University of Leeds)
  - Nationality restrictions: Leeds staff
  - Prior grant restrictions: none
  - Other: For early-stage collaborative projects; supports development of larger external grant applications
- **Themes/priorities:** Cross-faculty arts and humanities research with interdisciplinary potential
- **What they're looking for:** Collaborative initiatives that could generate external funding applications (AHRC, Leverhulme, BA, etc.)
- **Application process:** Internal — contact LAHRI
- **URL:** https://ahc.leeds.ac.uk/
- **Notes:** Useful for seeding a workshop or network that could support a Leverhulme Research Project Grant or AHRC application. Low amounts but quick to access.
