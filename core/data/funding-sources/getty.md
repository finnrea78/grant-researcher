## Scheme: Getty Scholars Program

- **Status:** rolling
- **Deadline:** TBC — typically opens annually in September–October for the following academic year. See URL for current cycle.
- **Amount:** varies — stipend (amount not stated on harvested page; past amounts have been approximately $65,000–$70,000 for a full year)
- **Duration:** Academic year (September–June) or semester
- **Career stage:** established
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: no (international applicants eligible)
  - Nationality restrictions: none stated
  - Prior grant restrictions: none stated
  - Other: Residential programme in Los Angeles. Full-time residency required.
- **Themes/priorities:** Art history and visual culture. Theme changes annually — applicants must demonstrate alignment with current year's theme. Check URL for current theme before applying.
- **What they're looking for:** Established scholars conducting innovative research in art history or visual culture. Residential community of international scholars, artists, and critics.
- **Application process:** see https://www.getty.edu/projects/getty-scholars-program/
- **URL:** https://www.getty.edu/projects/getty-scholars-program/
- **Notes:** One of the most prestigious residential fellowships in art history globally. Requires full-time residency in Los Angeles — significant commitment. Annual theme alignment is critical and must be checked before applying.

## Scheme: Library Research Grants

- **Status:** rolling
- **Deadline:** TBC — see URL
- **Amount:** varies — travel and accommodation for research visits to the Getty Research Institute Library
- **Duration:** Short-term visits
- **Career stage:** any
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: no (international applicants eligible)
  - Nationality restrictions: none stated
  - Prior grant restrictions: none stated
  - Other: Must demonstrate specific need for Getty Library holdings not available elsewhere
- **Themes/priorities:** Art history and visual culture. Must be tied to specific Getty collections.
- **What they're looking for:** Scholars with a defined research need for resources at the Getty Research Institute Library and Special Collections
- **Application process:** see https://www.getty.edu/projects/library-research-grants/
- **URL:** https://www.getty.edu/projects/library-research-grants/
- **Notes:** Lower prestige but lower competition than residential fellowships. Investigate whether the Getty Research Library holds relevant African art history materials (e.g. Paul Tishman collection documentation, historic exhibition records) before applying.
