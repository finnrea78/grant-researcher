## Scheme: Research and Travel Grants

- **Status:** open
- **Deadline:** Spring and Autumn rounds — next deadline TBC (Spring 2025 round listed as March 2025; verify current round at URL)
- **Amount:** Up to £2,500
- **Duration:** Not specified
- **Career stage:** established
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: no (individual sculpture historians, academics, and conservators eligible)
  - Nationality restrictions: none stated
  - Prior grant restrictions: none stated
  - Other: Cannot fund work that informs or develops an artist's own practice. Individuals only (not organisations).
- **Themes/priorities:** Research in sculpture conservation, production, history, and/or interpretation. Broad definition of sculpture.
- **What they're looking for:** Individual researchers needing funding for travel, archive visits, or other research activities related to sculpture history.
- **Application process:** Register via Flexi-Grant portal at https://henry-moore.flexigrant.com/
- **URL:** https://henry-moore.org/what-we-do/grants-fellowships/
- **Notes:** Most directly relevant scheme for an individual art history researcher. Foundation runs Spring and Autumn rounds annually. Rea has prior Henry Moore Foundation funding (£105,000 project grant, 2006–09) — this relationship is a positive signal.
