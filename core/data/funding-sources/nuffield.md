## Scheme: Main Grants (Research, Development and Analysis Fund)

- **Status:** open
- **Deadline:** 2026-04-13 (11:59pm — outline application)
- **Amount:** Up to £500,000
- **Duration:** Usually 6 months to 3 years
- **Career stage:** any
- **Eligibility:**
  - Must be based at UK HEI: not specified
  - Nationality restrictions: not specified
  - Prior grant restrictions: not specified
  - Other: see URL
- **Themes/priorities:** "Research projects that address one or more of our five interconnected priorities and aim to improve lives in the UK." The five priorities are: Education, Welfare, Justice, Democracy and participation, and Science in society.
- **What they're looking for:** Evidence-based research with clear UK social impact. Primarily social science and education focus.
- **Application process:** Outline application first, then invited full application.
- **URL:** https://www.nuffieldfoundation.org/funding-for-research/main-grants
- **Notes:** **Low relevance for arts and humanities research.** Nuffield's priorities are firmly in social policy, welfare, and education. An African art history or heritage project would need a strong "social impact in the UK" framing to be competitive. Not recommended as a primary target.
