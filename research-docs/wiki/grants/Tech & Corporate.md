# GrantResearcher: Tech Company & Corporate Research Grants

## BIG TECH RESEARCH AWARDS

These are often overlooked by academics but provide unrestricted funding with prestige.

### Google
- **Google Academic Research Awards**: Open to professors at degree-granting institutions. Multiple RFP cycles per year. Focus areas rotate (recently: AI for Privacy, Safety & Security). Unrestricted gifts. Proposals accepted from computer science, legal studies, public policy, social sciences, psychology, HCI.
- **Google Research Scholar Program**: For early-career faculty (within 5 years of first academic position). Up to $60K unrestricted.
- **Google PhD Fellowships**: Named fellowships for outstanding PhD students. ~$30-50K/year.
- **Google.org Impact Challenge**: Grants for social impact projects using AI/tech.

### Microsoft
- **Microsoft Research Fellowship 2026**: Faculty, PhD students, postdocs. Collaboration with MSR. Region-based funding: $47K (US/Canada), $27K (Europe), $17K (Africa, Asia-Pac, LATAM). Open to Africa, Australia/NZ, Canada, Europe, India, Hong Kong, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Taiwan, Latin America, US. Deadline typically December.
- **Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship**: For PhD students working on computing research.
- **Microsoft AI for Good**: Grants for AI in sustainability, accessibility, humanitarian action, cultural heritage.

### Meta (Facebook)
- **Meta Research PhD Fellowship**: 2 years paid tuition + $42K/year stipend. Specific research areas each cycle (AR/VR, AI, security, HCI etc.). Annual summit at Meta HQ.
- **Meta Research Awards**: Faculty research awards in specific areas.

### Amazon
- **Amazon Research Awards (ARA)**: Unrestricted cash awards + AWS Promotional Credits. Academic institutions and non-profits. Focus areas: ML, robotics, NLP, computer vision, sustainability, operations research. Founded 2015, merged with AWS MLRA in 2020.
- **Amazon Science Hub**: Collaborative research programmes.

### Apple
- **Apple Scholars in AI/ML**: PhD students. Funding + mentorship + Apple internship.

### Other tech
- **NVIDIA Academic Hardware Grants**: GPU donations for research.
- **IBM Research Awards**: Various faculty and student programmes.
- **Cisco Research Grants**: Networking, security, AI.
- **Salesforce Research**: AI research grants.
- **DeepMind Scholarships**: For underrepresented groups in STEM.
- **Huawei HIRP (Innovation Research Program)**: Open to universities globally.

## PHARMA / BIOTECH INDUSTRY GRANTS

Pharmaceutical companies invest billions in research, usually product development but also:

- **Investigator-Initiated Studies (IIS)**: Most major pharma companies (Pfizer, Roche, Novartis, AstraZeneca, GSK, J&J, Merck) have programmes where academic researchers can propose studies.
- **Pfizer Global Medical Grants**: Competitive grants for independent clinical research.
- **Roche/Genentech Grants**: Research collaboration opportunities.
- **AstraZeneca Independent Research**: Academic collaborations.
- **GSK Academic Partnerships**: Open innovation challenges.
- **Wellcome Trust / Industry partnerships**: Various co-funded schemes.

## CORPORATE FOUNDATIONS

- **Siemens Foundation**: STEM education, workforce development.
- **Shell Foundation**: Energy access, sustainable mobility.
- **Unilever Foundation**: Hygiene, sanitation, nutrition.
- **IKEA Foundation**: Climate, livelihoods, refugees.
- **Bloomberg Philanthropies**: Environment, public health, education, arts.
- **Mastercard Foundation**: African development, financial inclusion, education. Fully-funded scholarship programmes.

## UK INNOVATION / INDUSTRY

- **Innovate UK**: Part of UKRI. Grants for businesses (including with academic partners). Smart Grants, Biomedical Catalyst, Industrial Strategy Challenge Fund.
- **Knowledge Transfer Partnerships (KTP)**: Government-funded partnerships between businesses and universities.
- **Catapult Centres**: Network of technology and innovation centres offering collaborative R&D opportunities.
- **InnovateUK EDGE**: Business growth support with access to grants.

## KEY INSIGHT FOR GRANTRESEARCHER

Tech company grants are a **massive blind spot** for most academics outside CS/engineering. A humanities researcher might not know that:
- Microsoft Research Fellowship accepts proposals from legal studies and social sciences
- Google Academic Research Awards welcome public policy and psychology
- These are often **easier to get** than UKRI grants (smaller applicant pools for non-CS academics)
- They provide unrestricted funding with no FEC complications

GrantResearcher should actively match non-CS researchers to tech company grants where their research touches AI, data, society, ethics, digital culture, etc.
