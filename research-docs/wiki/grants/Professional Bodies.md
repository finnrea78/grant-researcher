# GrantResearcher: Professional Bodies, Learned Societies & Discipline-Specific Grants

## THE LONG TAIL — WHY THIS MATTERS

There are hundreds of professional bodies and learned societies in the UK alone, many with their own small grant schemes (£500-£10,000). Individually they're modest, but collectively they represent a massive untapped funding layer. Most researchers don't know what's available outside their immediate field.

## DISCOVERY STRATEGY FOR GRANTRESEARCHER

Most professional bodies publish their grant schemes on their websites but don't advertise widely. Strategy:

1. **Build a registry** of all UK professional bodies (start with Royal Charter bodies, then expand). The Euraxess UK page lists many.
2. **Monitor each body's grants/awards page** on a scheduled scrape (monthly is sufficient — these change slowly).
3. **Tag by discipline, career stage, and grant size** for matching.
4. **Alert researchers** when a grant from their discipline's professional body is open — many don't even know their own society has grants.

The value proposition: "Did you know the Prehistoric Society has a Research Fund grant you're eligible for?"
