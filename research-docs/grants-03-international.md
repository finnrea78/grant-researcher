# GrantResearcher: International Grant Sources (All Geographies)

## NORTH AMERICA

### United States — Federal
- **Grants.gov / Simpler.Grants.gov**: All US federal grants across 26 agencies. **REST API available** (early dev). Free, searchable.
- **NSF (National Science Foundation)**: All STEM + social sciences. International collaboration programmes including Global Centers ($5M awards, partnering with UKRI, JSPS, NRF Korea, NSERC/SSHRC Canada). NSF AI Institutes ($140M available).
- **NIH (National Institutes of Health)**: 27 institutes. International collaborations funded. NIH RePORT for portfolio analysis.
- **NEH (National Endowment for the Humanities)**: US federal humanities funder. International partnerships including with AHRC.
- **NEA (National Endowment for the Arts)**: Arts project funding.
- **Department of Defense (DARPA, AFOSR, ONR, ARO)**: Blue-sky research. XAI, AIE, Machine Common Sense programmes.
- **NASA**: Space science, earth science, astrophysics research grants.
- **Department of Energy (DOE)**: Energy research, national labs.
- **Fulbright Commission**: UK-US research exchanges and fellowships.

### United States — Foundations
- **Getty Foundation / Getty Research Institute**: Art history, conservation, visual culture. Scholars programme (residential), library research grants, Connecting Art Histories, AAAHI (African American Art History Initiative). $5K-$240K+.
- **Andrew W. Mellon Foundation**: Major humanities funder globally. Arts, higher education, scholarly communications. Conservation Initiative in African Art ($1.5M).
- **Ford Foundation**: Social justice, arts, civic engagement. Searchable grants database. Africa, Latin America focus.
- **MacArthur Foundation**: Climate, criminal justice, nuclear challenges, technology in public interest.
- **Rockefeller Foundation**: Health, food, energy, economic opportunity.
- **Carnegie Corporation of New York**: Education, democracy, international peace. Africa programme.
- **Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation**: Global health, development, education.
- **Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI)**: Biomedical research. Investigator programme.
- **Simons Foundation**: Mathematics, physics, life sciences, autism research.
- **Alfred P. Sloan Foundation**: Science, technology, economics, education.
- **John Templeton Foundation**: Science & religion, philosophy, character virtue. Large grants (often $500K+). Open to international applicants.
- **Spencer Foundation**: Education research. International.
- **Henry Luce Foundation**: Asia, theology, arts, higher education.
- **Wenner-Gren Foundation**: Anthropology research. International. Small grants and fellowships.

**Data access**: Foundation Directory Online (Candid) — 312,000+ grantmakers. Industry standard. Subscription. IRS Form 990s are public data for US foundations (gives grant history). Candid/GuideStar merged. GrantWatch, Instrumentl also aggregate US data.

### Canada
- **NSERC (Natural Sciences & Engineering Research Council)**: Canada's main STEM funder. Discovery grants, strategic partnerships, alliance grants.
- **SSHRC (Social Sciences & Humanities Research Council)**: Insight grants, partnership grants, connection grants. International joint initiatives with AHRC, ESRC. Open Research Area (ORA) with France, Germany, Netherlands.
- **CIHR (Canadian Institutes of Health Research)**: Health research across 13 institutes.
- **Canada Research Chairs**: Tier 1 ($200K/year, 7 years) and Tier 2 ($100K/year, 5 years).
- **Mitacs**: International research internships and collaborations.
- **CIFAR (Canadian Institute for Advanced Research)**: Networks, fellowships, global scholars.
- **Tri-Agency (NSERC/SSHRC/CIHR)**: Joint programmes, New Frontiers in Research Fund.

## EUROPE

### EU-wide (Horizon Europe)
See previous file. Key additions:
- **ERC grants**: Starting (€1.5M), Consolidator (€2M), Advanced (€2.5M), Synergy (€10M for 2-4 PIs). UK fully associated since Jan 2024.
- **Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA)**: Postdoctoral fellowships (€188K+), Doctoral Networks, Staff Exchanges, COFUND. UK eligible.
- **European Innovation Council (EIC)**: Pathfinder (€3-4M), Transition, Accelerator (up to €17.5M equity+grant).

### Germany
- **DFG (Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft)**: Germany's main research funder. Individual grants, collaborative research centres, research training groups. Some programmes open to international collaboration. Partners with ESRC, AHRC on bilateral calls.
- **Alexander von Humboldt Foundation**: Research fellowships for experienced researchers (any nationality). Humboldt Research Awards (€60K). Extremely prestigious.
- **DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service)**: Comprehensive scholarship/fellowship database. Many open to UK researchers.
- **Gerda Henkel Foundation**: Historical humanities. Any nationality. €2,760/month postdoc, €3,720/month senior. Rolling applications. Special programmes: Democracy, Forced Migration, Lost Cities, Patrimonies. Lisa Maskell Fellowships for Africa.
- **Volkswagen Foundation (VolkswagenStiftung)**: One of Europe's largest private science funders. Challenges for Europe, AI & society, planetary health.
- **Fritz Thyssen Foundation**: History, language & culture, philosophy & theology, social sciences.
- **Max Planck Society**: Guest researcher programmes.

### France
- **ANR (Agence Nationale de la Recherche)**: France's main research funder. Bilateral programmes with UKRI, DFG, JSPS.
- **CNRS**: Visiting researcher positions.

### Netherlands
- **NWO (Dutch Research Council)**: Veni/Vidi/Vici career grants. Open Research Area partner.

### Switzerland
- **SNSF (Swiss National Science Foundation)**: Project grants, fellowships. Ambizione, Eccellenza, PRIMA programmes.

### Nordics
- **NordForsk**: Joint Nordic research programmes. Arctic research with SSHRC.
- **Research Council of Finland**: Bilateral with NSF, UKRI.
- **Swedish Research Council (Vetenskapsrådet)**
- **Research Council of Norway**

### Other European
- **FWF (Austrian Science Fund)**: Research grants, Hertha Firnberg/Elise Richter career programmes.
- **Czech Science Foundation (GAČR)**: International calls.
- **EUI (European University Institute)**: Florence. Fellowships, grants. Administers ESA history grants.

## ASIA-PACIFIC

### Japan
- **JSPS (Japan Society for the Promotion of Science)**: Japan's leading funding agency. Bilateral joint research with ESRC, SSHRC, DFG, ANR. Postdoctoral fellowships for foreign researchers. KAKENHI (Grants-in-Aid for Scientific Research). HOPE Meetings with Nobel Laureates (Asia-Pacific + Africa). A3 Foresight (Japan-China-Korea).
- **JST (Japan Science and Technology Agency)**: ASPIRE for Rising Scientists (ECR). SICORP strategic international collaborative research. Partner in NSF Global Centers.
- **JSPS London Office**: jsps.org — UK-specific information.

### South Korea
- **NRF (National Research Foundation of Korea)**: All disciplines. Partner in NSF Global Centers. Bilateral programmes.
- **KIST (Korea Institute of Science and Technology)**: Visiting researcher.

### Australia
- **ARC (Australian Research Council)**: Discovery projects, future fellowships, linkage projects, centres of excellence.
- **CSIRO**: Partner in NSF Global Centers. Applied research.
- **NHMRC (National Health and Medical Research Council)**: Health research. Investigator grants.

### China
- **NSFC (National Natural Science Foundation of China)**: Major funder. Some bilateral programmes with UKRI, but political sensitivities around Trusted Research.
- **Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS)**: Visiting fellowships.

### India
- **DST (Department of Science and Technology)**: Various schemes.
- **ICSSR (Indian Council of Social Science Research)**: Social sciences.
- **UGC (University Grants Commission)**: Higher education research.

### Singapore
- **NRF Singapore**: Research grants, fellowships.
- **A*STAR**: Science and engineering research.

### Taiwan
- **NSTC (National Science and Technology Council)**: Research grants.

## MIDDLE EAST

- **Qatar National Research Fund (QNRF)**: Established 2006. $600M+ awarded across 700+ projects. National Priorities Research Program (NPRP) — average $1M, up to $5M. Covers engineering, life sciences, medicine, humanities, social sciences, arts. Requires Qatar-based partner. Recipients from 60+ countries.
- **KAUST (King Abdullah University of Science and Technology)**: Saudi Arabia. $20bn endowment. Competitive research grants, visiting fellowships. STEM-focused.
- **UAE research funds**: Various university-based (NYU Abu Dhabi, Khalifa University).
- **TÜBİTAK (Turkey)**: Scientific and technical research council. Bilateral with EU, partnerships with QF.

## AFRICA

- **African Academy of Sciences (AAS)**: Pan-African. Alliance for Accelerating Excellence in Science in Africa (AESA). Grand Challenges Africa.
- **National Research Foundation (South Africa)**: All disciplines. Next-gen, emerging, established researcher grants.
- **Stellenbosch Institute for Advanced Study (STIAS)**: Residential fellowships.
- **African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS)**: Mathematics research.
- **Carnegie Corporation of New York — Africa programmes**: Higher education, governance, peacebuilding.
- **Wellcome Trust — Africa programmes**: DELTAS Africa (research leadership), African Institutions Initiative.
- **UK FCDO / Newton Fund / GCRF**: Development-linked research partnerships.

## LATIN AMERICA

- **FAPESP (São Paulo Research Foundation, Brazil)**: Large state funder. Bilateral with UKRI, NSF.
- **CNPq (Brazil)**: National Council for Scientific and Technological Development.
- **CONICET (Argentina)**: National scientific research council.
- **CONACYT/CONAHCYT (Mexico)**: National council for science and technology.
- **Colciencias/MinCiencias (Colombia)**: Science and technology ministry.

## GLOBAL / MULTILATERAL

- **Human Frontier Science Program (HFSP)**: International. Interdisciplinary life sciences + physics/maths/CS/engineering. Research grants, fellowships. Post-doc level upwards.
- **WHO / TDR**: Tropical disease research.
- **World Bank**: Research grants, development research.
- **UNESCO**: Various small programmes.
- **CRDF Global**: Science and engineering addressing societal needs.
- **IREX (International Research and Exchanges Board)**: Technology for development, conflict resolution, media.
- **IIE (Institute of International Education)**: Administers Fulbright and other exchange programmes.
