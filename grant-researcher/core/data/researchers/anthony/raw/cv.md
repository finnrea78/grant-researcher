# Professor Anthony G. Constantinides

**BSc(Eng), PhD, CEng, PACAD, FIEE, FIEEE, FRSA, FREng, FIET**

*Emeritus Professor of Signal Processing*
Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Imperial College London

**Institution:** Imperial College London, South Kensington, London SW7 2AZ
**Website:** www.fsplab.com

## Research Interests

Digital signal processing and filter design; communications systems; financial signal processing and engineering. Over 40 years of active research spanning both theoretical foundations and practical applications across these fields.

## Professional Service and Leadership

- First President, European Association for Signal Processing (EURASIP)
- Contributed to the establishment of the European Journal for Signal Processing
- Member of the Board of Governors, IEEE Signal Processing Society
- Member of multiple technical committees of the IEEE and the IEE
- Editorial board member of professional journals
- Organiser of the first international series of meetings on digital signal processing (London, 1967; Florence with Professor Vito Cappellini, 1972 onwards)
- Technical programme committee member for numerous IEEE, IEE, and other international conferences

---

## Publications

Author of several books and over 250 papers in learned journals in the areas of digital signal processing, communications, and financial signal processing.
