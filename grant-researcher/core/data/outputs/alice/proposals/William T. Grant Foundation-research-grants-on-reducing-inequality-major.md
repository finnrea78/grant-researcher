## 7. Budget Considerations

The William T. Grant Foundation funds research costs including personnel (research assistants, postdocs), participant compensation, data collection costs, travel for fieldwork, and dissemination activities. Indirect costs are capped at 15% of the total award. For a Major grant, budgets typically support at least one significant personnel line (e.g., a postdoctoral researcher or project coordinator) plus data collection and analysis costs. The Foundation does not typically fund dissertation research or tuition — this is a research project grant, not a training fellowship. If Alicia is positioned as a research team member rather than PI, the budget should reflect appropriate compensation for her role. Equipment, software licences (e.g., qualitative analysis tools, statistical packages), and school-based fieldwork costs (travel, materials, participant incentives) are all reasonable line items.
